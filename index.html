Frequently asked questions

What is CableMatch?

CableMatch is a free educational guide that helps people understand different cables, connectors and their uses.

Are USB-C cables all the same?

No. USB-C describes the connector shape. Different cables can support different charging power, data speeds and video features.

Can HDMI and DisplayPort do the same thing?

Both can carry digital video and audio to displays, but they use different connector standards and have different features.

Does every cable work with every device?

No. You need to consider connector shape, supported standards, power requirements and device compatibility.

Is CableMatch free?

Yes. The information in this version of CableMatch is free to browse.

Compatibility reminder: Connector shape alone does not guarantee compatibility. Check your device's specifications before purchasing or connecting cables.
